# Mighty Transcriber Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Mighty Transcriber is developed by Cristian Zeni ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or contact us for support.

## Our approach to privacy

Mighty Transcriber processes your audio and text on your iPhone. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings or transcripts, require an app account, include advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information.

Local processing and storage are necessary to provide the app's features. This is different from collecting your recordings, documents, or usage information on our servers.

## Audio transcription and Apple Intelligence

Speech transcription takes place on your device using Apple's on-device speech technologies. The app does not upload your recordings or transcripts to us or to a remote transcription service.

When available, Apple Intelligence can improve punctuation, capitalization, and paragraph structure using a local language model. This processing also takes place on your device.

Apple Intelligence is optional. If it is unavailable, the app can continue transcribing and saving text without the additional revision step. It does not switch to a cloud AI service.

Apple's speech and language models may need to be downloaded before local processing becomes available. Availability depends on your device, language, and system configuration.

## Microphone and file access

Mighty Transcriber requests microphone permission when you use voice recording. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. Without microphone access, you can still import supported audio files.

When you import a file, the app accesses the item you select through the system file picker and creates a local working copy for transcription. Importing does not delete the original file.

If you select a file stored with iCloud Drive or another cloud provider, that provider may need to download it to your device.

## Local documents and recovery data

Saved text documents and document exports are stored in the app's local Documents folder, accessible through **Files > On My iPhone > Mighty Transcriber**. You decide which documents to create, organize, export, share, or delete.

Searching document titles and text contents takes place locally.

The app also stores local session information, transcription progress, and temporary audio to support processing and recovery after an interruption. Temporary audio and imported working copies are normally removed after a transcription completes or the session is discarded. Paused or interrupted sessions may retain recovery data until you resume or delete them.

## Preferences, statistics, and diagnostics

The app stores preferences and usage statistics locally, including transcription time and information used to display document counts and storage usage. These statistics are not sent to us.

The app may write technical diagnostic entries to the device's system logs. These entries do not include audio recordings or transcript contents. We do not include a remote analytics or crash-reporting service.

## Exporting, sharing, and backups

**Settings > Export all** creates a ZIP archive containing the files and subfolders in your local document archive. You choose where to save or share it using the system sharing interface.

This export contains your document files; it does not include internal recovery data or temporary transcription audio. Temporary ZIP files created for sharing are normally removed when the sharing interface is dismissed.

Mighty Transcriber does not provide its own iCloud document synchronization. Its document archive and internal recovery/audio storage are marked as excluded from system backups. Export important documents yourself rather than relying on a device backup to preserve them.

If you save or share documents with another app, storage provider, or recipient, that destination handles the copies under its own policies. Copies stored outside Mighty Transcriber may be synchronized or backed up by the service you choose.

## Purchases and Apple services

Apple processes App Store payments. We do not receive your payment card details.

Apple also handles App Store downloads, system model downloads, and any system diagnostics you choose to share under its own terms and [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support communications

If you email **mightypdf@protonmail.com**, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues.

Our email provider, Proton Mail, processes these communications as part of delivering its email service. See the [Proton Mail Privacy Policy](https://proton.me/mail/privacy-policy).

Please avoid sending sensitive recordings, transcripts, or documents unless they are necessary for your request.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access to or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable legal requirements.

## Deleting your information

You can delete individual documents in the app or use **Settings > Delete all data and reset counters** to remove the local document archive, recovery data, and usage counters.

Deleting local data does not delete original audio files imported from another location, copies you previously shared or saved elsewhere, or copies held in external backups.

We cannot access, retrieve, or delete information stored only on your device. Export important documents before resetting the app or uninstalling it. Manage external copies through the relevant apps or services.

For information you have sent to us by email, contact the support address below.
