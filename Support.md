# Mighty Transcriber Support

For any technical support request, question, or issue with Mighty Transcriber, contact the app's developer, **Cristian Zeni**, by email:

**[mightypdf@protonmail.com](mailto:mightypdf@protonmail.com)**

Please include **Mighty Transcriber** in the email subject so we can identify the app your request concerns.

## How to request help

Describe the issue and, if possible, include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The Mighty Transcriber version, shown in the app's Settings.
- The steps that led to the issue and what you expected to happen.
- Whether you were recording with the microphone or importing an audio file.
- The transcription language and, for imported audio, the file format and approximate duration.
- Any error message or a screenshot that helps explain the problem.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or sensitive recordings and transcripts. Hide personal or confidential information in screenshots before sending them.

## Transcription and Apple Intelligence

Transcription requires Apple's on-device speech support for your device and language. An internet connection may be needed to download the required speech assets before first use.

Apple Intelligence is optional. If it is unavailable, transcription can continue without the additional text revision step. You can check the availability of local transcription and Apple Intelligence in the app's Settings.

## Protecting your documents

Before resetting the app or uninstalling it, use **Settings > Export all** to save a ZIP copy of your document archive through the system sharing interface.

The ZIP contains your document files and folders, but not unfinished transcription sessions or temporary audio.

We cannot retrieve recordings or documents stored only on your device.
